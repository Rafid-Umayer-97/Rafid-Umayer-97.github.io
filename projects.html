Systems portfolio

Projects that connect theory, data, and deployment.

A curated set of wireless, sensing, and edge-AI systems built across UIUC, InterDigital, UT Dallas, and BUET-JIDPUS.

WiNeRF

Hardware-constrained neural radiance fields for actionable wireless channel modeling from sparse commodity WiFi CSI. Accepted to EWSN 2026.

I-NAV

Inverse navigation for questionnaire-based geolocation in GPS-denied environments, accepted to Elsevier’s Array.

Weather-aware latency prediction

Predictive models relating rainfall, snow, humidity, and pressure to wireless network KPIs on the NSF PAWR ARA testbed.

Earthquake early warning

Self-supervised contrastive GNNs for real-time seismic intensity prediction from short waveform windows.

6G beamforming and RIS

Low-complexity algorithms and learning models for MU-MIMO beam selection, THz robustness, and metasurface-assisted interference suppression.

Automated level crossing

Raspberry-Pi computer vision prototype for train detection, servo-controlled barriers, and low-cost edge AI safety infrastructure.
