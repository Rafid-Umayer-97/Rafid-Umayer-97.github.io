Projects

Selected work translating physics-informed AI, signal processing, and edge intelligence into wireless systems, sensing applications, and safety-critical prototypes.

Wireless NeRFEWSN 2026

WiNeRF wireless channel modeling

Designed for actionable wireless digital twins, WiNeRF learns complex-valued CSI fields from sparse measurements while incorporating antenna-array observability through cone-based sampling.

GPS-deniedArray

I-NAV inverse navigation

Developed an inverse geolocation framework that infers possible locations from questionnaire-based travel cues and operates fully offline when GPS is unavailable.

ARA testbedNetwork analytics

Weather-aware latency prediction

Analyzed how rainfall, snow, humidity, pressure, and other environmental variables influence wireless latency using NSF PAWR ARA experiments and weather station data.

IEEE TGRSGNN

Earthquake early warning

Built a self-supervised contrastive graph neural network for real-time seismic intensity prediction, supporting warning decisions from short waveform windows.

Edge AIRaspberry Pi

Automated level crossing system

Implemented a Raspberry-Pi computer vision prototype for train detection, alarms, and barrier control at unmanned crossings.

MIMORISTHz

Beamforming and metasurface intelligence

Developed deep learning, greedy optimization, singular-vector projection, and meta-learning methods for massive MIMO, THz robustness, and reconfigurable intelligent surfaces.
