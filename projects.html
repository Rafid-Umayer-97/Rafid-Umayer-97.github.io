Projects

Selected research systems and applied projects. For a deeper discussion of current research directions, see the Research page.

RayNO — high-fidelity radio-map prediction

A physics-unrolled neural operator for learning radio maps from low-cost simulation. The research combines physics-aware supervision, field reconstruction and numerical evaluation against high-fidelity ray tracing.

NeurIPS 2026 paper · Research details

GCNO — physics-based CSI feedback

A variable-rate representation using dominant multipath directions and gains, with an analytically defined decoder. Developed during my InterDigital AI Lab internship, and evaluated on city-scale ray-traced wireless channels.

arXiv preprint · Research details

WiNeRF — channel fields from commodity Wi-Fi

A continuous, complex-valued wireless channel field derived from sparse CSI. WiNeRF explicitly accounts for antenna resolution and phase uncertainty, and allows downstream tasks such as angle-of-arrival estimation and beamforming.

EWSN 2026 paper

FoliaRT — neural canopy scattering

A collaborative model for predicting frequency-dependent directional canopy scattering and transmission, to replace coarse foliage attenuation assumptions in ray tracing. Submitted to ACM SenSys 2027.

Research summary

Earthquake early warning

Seismic intensity prediction from short, early waveforms using a self-supervised contrastive GNN. The model uses spatiotemporal relationships across sensor stations and was evaluated on real earthquake datasets.

IEEE TGRS 2024 paper

WiViz — wireless literacy research

Collaborative research exploring whether visualizing radio propagation mechanisms helps non-experts form better mental models of everyday wireless behavior. Manuscript under review at HotMobile 2027.

Millimeter-wave radar sensing

A signal-processing and CNN framework for contactless heart-rate estimation from millimeter-wave radar ballistocardiography. Published in Array (2023).
