Selected projects

Research systems with measurable impact.

A closer look at the models, experiments and practical systems behind my publications. Each entry links to the original paper or its research explanation.

NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted

RayNO — Physics-unrolled neural operators

A scientific ML model that turns inexpensive ray-tracing outputs into high-fidelity wireless fields. Physics-aligned stages learn reflection, diffraction, and scattering; a denoising theorem explains learning from imperfect simulation labels.

~250× faster high-fidelity field prediction

AAAI 2027 · Under review

GCNO — Compact physical channel models

A physics-structured neural operator that extracts path-based representations of complex wireless channels, reconstructs them analytically, and transfers to antenna configurations never seen during training.

~⅓ of feedback bits at comparable accuracy
Read preprintResearch details
EWSN 2026 · Published

WiNeRF — Learning from measured signals

A measurement-constrained neural field built from sparse commodity Wi-Fi CSI, enabling continuous channel prediction and downstream beamforming, AoA estimation, and coverage mapping.

4.9 dB higher prediction SNR on average
Read paperResearch details
SenSys 2027 · Submitted

FoliaRT — Learning vegetation scattering

A reusable neural electromagnetic canopy surrogate that captures directional scattering instead of relying on simple attenuation-only foliage models, with simulation and real-world validation.

53% lower median received-power error in a field study
