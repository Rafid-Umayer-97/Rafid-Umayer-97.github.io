Hobbies

Field days

When I’m not buried in equations and code, you’ll usually find me chasing a ball or smacking it over the boundary. Outdoor sports are my reset button, with cricket, soccer, and badminton topping the list.

In cricket, I’m a left-handed opening batsman and a right-arm medium pacer, always trying to get my team off to a flying start in the powerplay. On the soccer field I bounce between the left and right wing, running perhaps more enthusiastically than skillfully, but always in the thick of the play.

  • Technically Solid Batsman
  • Aggressive Opener
  • Flying Winger trying to emulate CR7

Board battles

Chess is my indoor obsession. Despite never having formal training, I’ve clawed my way to ~2100 on chess.com and ~2300 on lichess, occasionally stealing a win from an International Master when the tactics flow.

It’s equal parts hobby and addiction, a mix of late-night puzzles, scrappy bullet games, and the occasional celebratory screenshot. Blitz, bullet, rapid, I’m game for any of the faster formats that lets me avoid theory and preparation.

Rapid peak: 2257 (lichess) Blitz peak: 2353 (lichess) Bullet peak: 2391 (lichess)
