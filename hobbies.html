Hobbies

A glimpse into the creative and personal interests that keep me energized outside the lab.

🏏

Field days

When I’m not buried in equations and code, you’ll usually find me chasing a ball—or smacking it over the boundary. Outdoor sports are my reset button, with cricket, soccer, and badminton topping the list.

In cricket, I’m a left-handed opening batsman and a right-arm medium pacer, always hunting for that early over six in the powerplay. On the soccer field I bounce between the left and right wing, running perhaps more enthusiastically than skillfully, but always in the thick of the play.

  • Weekend nets warrior
  • Powerplay boundary hunter
  • Wing sprint enthusiast
♟️

Board battles

Chess is my indoor obsession. Despite never having formal training, I’ve clawed my way to ~2100 on chess.com and ~2300 on lichess—occasionally stealing a win from an International Master when the tactics flow.

It’s equal parts hobby and addiction, a mix of late-night puzzles, scrappy rapid matches, and the occasional celebratory screenshot. Blitz, bullet, classical—I’m game for any format that lets me push a pawn with style.

Rapid peak: 2100 (chess.com) Blitz peak: 2300 (lichess) IM scalps: rare but real!
