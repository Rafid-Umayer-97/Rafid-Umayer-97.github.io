Current profile

Researcher at the intersection of wireless systems, machine learning, and physical modeling.

I completed my first year in the UIUC Computer Science PhD program with a perfect 4.0/4.0 GPA after finishing my thesis-based M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at UT Dallas, also with a 4.0/4.0 GPA. I previously received my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from BUET and worked on earthquake early warning at BUET-JIDPUS.

My research agenda centers on building reliable AI for physical wireless environments: hardware-constrained neural radiance fields for CSI reconstruction, hybrid neural operators for wireless field modeling, low-complexity beamforming, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, integrated sensing and communications, and contactless sensing.

WiNeRF: hardware-constrained wireless radiance fields

WiNeRF replaces idealized line-ray wireless NeRF sampling with cone-based support regions that reflect finite antenna-array angular observability, enabling complex CSI reconstruction and actionable downstream beamforming, AoA estimation, and RSSI mapping.

ArrayGPS-deniedGeolocation

I-NAV: inverse navigation without GPS

I-NAV estimates feasible geographic regions from questionnaire-style travel information, matching commercial routing engines within minutes while operating offline for GPS-denied environments.
