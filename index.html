Biography

From signal processing foundations to wireless foundation models

I received my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from the Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET), where my thesis explored deep learning–based hybrid beamforming for MIMO systems. I later completed an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering at The University of Texas at Dallas with a 4.00/4.00 GPA, researching low-latency beamforming and metasurface design for 6G wireless networks.

I am now pursuing my PhD in Computer Science at UIUC as a Siebel School Fellowship recipient. My research focuses on physics-informed AI for wireless systems: neural radio fields, hybrid neural operators, RF digital twins, MU-MIMO beamforming, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, and integrated sensing-communication.
