Rafid Umayer Murshed

PhD Candidate in Computer Science · University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign

I am a PhD candidate in the Siebel School of Computing and Data Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Elahé Soltanaghai. I work on scientific machine learning for wireless systems, especially physics-informed neural operators, neural fields, and efficient physical simulation.

My research asks how physical structure can help machine learning models recover useful information from sparse measurements and imperfect simulations. My recent first-author work at NeurIPS 2026 develops a physics-unrolled neural operator for high-fidelity ray tracing. I have also worked on channel modeling and compression at InterDigital AI Lab, where I was a Machine Learning Research Intern in summer–fall 2026.

I previously earned an M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Texas at Dallas (2025) and a B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from BUET (2022).

Research interests

I am broadly interested in physics-informed AI, scientific machine learning, numerical methods, neural operators, wireless propagation, and spatiotemporal learning. My research combines simulation, mathematical structure, and experimental data. I am also interested in efficient GPU computing and learned surrogates for physical systems.

At UIUC, I took CS 598: Scientific Machine Learning with Luke Olson and Matthew West, studying machine learning approaches to ODE/PDE models, numerical integration, and computational physics. My publications connect these tools to practical wireless and sensing problems.

Physics-Unrolled Neural Operator for Accelerating High-Fidelity Wireless Ray Tracing

Rafid Umayer Murshed, Saif Ur Rahman, Mingyue Tang, Elahé Soltanaghai

NeurIPS 2026 · Accepted

RayNO learns a high-fidelity radio map from inexpensive ray tracing and scene geometry. Its three operators refine reflection, diffraction and scattering; a theoretical result explains when it can learn from noisy intermediate-fidelity labels.

PaperResearch summary

GCNO: Gramian Chebyshev Neural Operator for Physics-Based Compression of Wireless Channels

Rafid Umayer Murshed, Shahab Hamidi-Rad, Elahé Soltanaghai, Akshay Malhotra

AAAI 2027 · Under review

A variable-rate neural operator represents a wireless channel using a small set of propagation paths. Channel reconstruction is analytic, and the model transfers to unseen antenna-array sizes without retraining.

PreprintResearch summary

WiNeRF: Measurement Constrained Radiance Fields for Actionable Wireless Channel Modeling

Saif Ur Rahman, Rafid Umayer Murshed, Anton Dmitriev, Cagri Tanriover, Rahul C. Shah, Elahé Soltanaghai

EWSN 2026 · Published

Measurement-constrained neural fields learn complex-valued CSI from sparse commodity Wi-Fi observations, without requiring 3D scene scans. The resulting field is reusable for beamforming, AoA estimation and coverage mapping.
