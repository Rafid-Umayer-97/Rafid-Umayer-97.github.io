Featured recent manuscripts

Neural wireless fields and environment-aware intelligence

  • WiNeRF: Hardware-Constrained Radiance Fields for Actionable Wireless Channel Modeling
    Accepted to EWSN 2026, Dresden, Germany — S. U. Rahman, R. U. Murshed, A. Dmitriev, C. Tanriover, R. C. Shah, E. Soltanaghai

    Learns spatially continuous, complex-valued wireless channel representations from sparse commodity WiFi CSI, outperforming neural baselines by 3–6.7 dB SNR and supporting beamforming, AoA, and RSSI mapping workflows.

    PDF
  • Hybrid Neural Operators for Wireless Field Modeling
    Submitted to NeurIPS 2026 — R. U. Murshed et al.

    Develops specialized neural operators for object-centric wireless field modeling, targeting generalization across environments and downstream integrated sensing-and-communication tasks.

  • I-NAV: Inverse Navigation for Questionnaire-Based Geolocation in GPS-Denied Environments
    Accepted to Array (Elsevier), 2025 — S. Qazi, J. Alejandro, R. U. Murshed, T. S. Evans, J. Lippert, M. Saquib

    Enables offline geolocation through inverse navigation, matching commercial routing engines within 2–3 minutes of travel-time accuracy and achieving strong spatial overlap with ORS regions.

  • MetaFAP: Meta-Learning for Frequency Agnostic Prediction of Metasurface Properties
    Accepted to IEEE Access pending minor revisions, 2025 — R. U. Murshed, M. S. A. Rafi, S. Reza, M. Saquib, I. Mahbub

    Predicts metasurface properties across frequencies with a meta-learning formulation, achieving MSE below 0.01 and strong correlation across datasets of varying difficulty.
