Research experience

Graduate Research Assistant — iSens Lab, UIUC

2025–present · Siebel School of Computing and Data Science

  • Developed a physics-unrolled neural operator for high-fidelity radio maps from low-cost ray tracing, with a denoising proof under conditionally unbiased simulation-label errors (NeurIPS 2026).
  • Developing temporal CSI feedback where a device and base station track propagation-path parameters and communicate updates; measured path representations required approximately 400× fewer numbers than full CSI.
  • Benchmarked path-parameter-based coordinated beamforming on NVIDIA L4, A100, and RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell GPUs, with 1.1–1.6× faster processing than optimized full-CSI calculations in few-path scenes.
  • Co-developed WiNeRF for measurement-based wireless radiance fields, and contributed to FoliaRT canopy scattering and WiViz wireless literacy research.

Graduate Research Assistant — University of Texas at Dallas

2023–2025 · Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering

  • Studied deep learning and signal processing for MU-MIMO beamforming, terahertz wireless communication, and metasurface design.
  • Coauthored seven papers, five as first author, and contributed technical research to NSF, NIH, FAA, and DARPA proposals.

Research Assistant — Japan Institute of Disaster Prevention and Urban Safety, BUET

2022–2023

  • Curated seismic waveform data from 48 earthquakes recorded by Bangladesh's national network and studied propagation, frequency content and attenuation in the Bengal Basin.
  • Developed spatiotemporal graph learning for early warning and seismic-intensity prediction (IEEE TGRS 2024).

Industry experience

Machine Learning Research Intern — InterDigital AI Lab, Los Altos, California

May–October 2026 · Supervisors: Akshay Malhotra and Shahab Hamidi-Rad

  • Developed GCNO, a variable-rate neural operator for path-based CSI compression with analytic reconstruction; matched a leading learned baseline with approximately one-third the feedback bits on a ray-traced city benchmark.
  • Demonstrated transfer to unseen antenna configurations without retraining and developed models predicting how mobile wireless channels evolve over time.
  • Contributed to a filed patent application for physics-based channel representation, compression, prediction and interoperability.
