Current research

Wireless foundation models grounded in physics and deployable hardware

Neural fields for RF channels

WiNeRF learns continuous, complex-valued wireless channel representations from sparse CSI while embedding antenna geometry, phase uncertainty, and limited spatial resolution into the model.

Hybrid neural operators

My NeurIPS 2026 submission studies hybrid neural operators for wireless field modeling, targeting fast generalization across scenes, objects, and propagation conditions.

AI-native 6G compression

At InterDigital AI Lab, I am working on unsupervised physics-based CSI compression and spatiotemporal channel forecasting for compact, actionable wireless digital twins.
